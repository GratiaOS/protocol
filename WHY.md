# WHY

Software has operating systems.

Organizations have processes.

Humans have language.

AI has prompts.

None of them have a shared instruction set.

Gratia is an attempt to define one.

---

A protocol for structured knowledge work.

Not a framework. Not a tool. An ISA — like x86 or ARM, but for the work that happens between thinking and doing.

Today the first runtime is AI models. Tomorrow it will be something else. The protocol remains.
